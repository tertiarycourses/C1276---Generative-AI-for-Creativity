# Labs — Generative AI for Creativity (C1276)

**Course Code:** C1276  |  **Version v1.0 · 27 July 2026**

All 11 labs build one connected **Island Botanica launch campaign**, which you begin in Lab 1 and finish in Lab 11 — from a blank page, through ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Copilot for ideas, voice, story and copy, into an AI image tool for a moodboard and on-brand visuals, and out as a social and launch content kit, a strengthened campaign concept and a rehearsed creative pitch. A Lumina Botanicals brief with the brand facts you need is supplied in `reference-pack/`; use your own non-confidential creative project wherever you prefer. There is **no assessment** — each lab verifies itself with a 'Test it' step.

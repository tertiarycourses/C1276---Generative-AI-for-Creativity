# Lumina Botanicals — Creative Brief

> **This is a fictional brand created for the C1276 course.** All names, products
> and details are invented for practice. Use this brief as the source material for
> your Island Botanica campaign, or substitute your own **non-confidential**
> creative project.

## The brand in one line

**Lumina Botanicals** is a Singapore artisanal home-fragrance and wellness brand —
small-batch candles, reed diffusers and room mists scented with **Southeast Asian
botanicals**.

## The brand story

- Founded **five years ago** by two friends who blended their first candles in a
  Tiong Bahru studio.
- Known for **natural wax**, hand-poured small batches, and scents rooted in the
  region's flora and memories of home.
- A loyal following built through weekend markets, a small online store, and a
  calm, considered social presence — not loud advertising.
- The brand feels **artisanal, botanical, wellness-minded and quietly premium** —
  it sells a moment of calm, not just a candle.

## The new launch — Island Botanica

A **limited-edition signature collection** of four candle scents inspired by
Southeast Asian flora, timed for the **year-end gifting and Lunar New Year**
season. It is the brand's most ambitious release, meant to win new customers and
delight existing ones with a giftable, collectible set.

| Scent | Inspiration | Mood |
|---|---|---|
| **Frangipani Dawn** | Frangipani (kampung) blossoms at first light | Soft, luminous, uplifting |
| **Pandan & Vetiver** | Pandan leaf and grounding vetiver root | Green, warm, grounding |
| **Kaffir Lime Grove** | Kaffir lime leaf and bright citrus | Fresh, zesty, awakening |
| **Ylang Midnight** | Ylang-ylang and warm woods after dark | Deep, sensual, calming |

## The audiences

1. **Premium gift-shoppers** — buying a thoughtful, beautiful present for family,
   friends or clients in the festive season; care about presentation, story and
   feeling generous.
2. **Wellness-seekers** — buying for their own calm, ritual and self-care; care
   about natural ingredients, mood, and a considered brand they trust.

## The brand voice (starting notes — you define the full guide in Lab 3)

- **Tone:** warm, sensory, calm, quietly luxurious — never loud or hard-selling.
- **Style:** short, evocative sentences; concrete sensory detail; a little
  poetry, but always clear.
- **Voice:** a knowledgeable, gentle guide who loves botany and craft — invites,
  never pushes.
- **We lean into:** the botanicals, the ritual, the sense of home and place,
  small-batch craft.
- **We avoid:** clichés ("elevate your space", "the perfect gift"), hype,
  exclamation marks, and any invented award or claim.

## The campaign goal (what the pack must do)

Create the **Island Botanica launch campaign pack** — a brand story, hero
taglines and product copy, copy adapted for both audiences and for each channel,
a visual moodboard and on-brand images, a social and launch content kit, a
bigger campaign concept, and a creative pitch — that tells the collection's story
and sells it into the year-end season, on a **modest small-brand budget**.

## Practical constraints

- **Modest budget:** favour ideas a small brand can actually run (organic social,
  in-store moments, small partnerships) over big-spend media.
- **Season:** the launch lands in the **year-end gifting / Lunar New Year** window.
- **Responsible use:** do **not** imitate any real brand's or living artist's
  protected look or voice; do not invent awards, ingredients or claims; be ready
  to disclose AI assistance where it matters (you decide the rules in Lab 4).

## The look (starting notes — you set the full direction in Lab 7)

Soft natural light, warm **earthy and botanical tones**, marble and linen
textures, tropical foliage, and a calm, editorial feel. Amber-glass vessels.
Everything should feel **hand-made and quietly premium**, not mass-produced.

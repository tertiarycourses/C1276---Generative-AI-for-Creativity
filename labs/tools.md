# Tools & Setup — Generative AI for Creativity (C1276)

Everything you need for the 11 hands-on labs. Every tool used in this course is
**web-based and cloud-powered** — there is nothing to install beyond a modern
browser. You use two kinds of tool together: **general chat assistants** for the
ideas, story and words, and an **AI image tool** for the visuals.

## Accounts & software

| Tool | What it's for | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Web browser** (Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox) | Runs every tool in this course | Keep it up to date |
| **A chat assistant** — ChatGPT (chat.openai.com), Claude (claude.ai), Gemini (gemini.google.com) or Microsoft Copilot (copilot.microsoft.com) | Ideation, brand voice, story, copy, adaptation, workflow, pitch | A free account for any one of them is enough; the trainer will confirm what's available |
| **An AI image tool** — DALL·E in ChatGPT, Gemini, Microsoft Copilot / Designer, Adobe Firefly (firefly.adobe.com) or Canva (canva.com) | Generating a moodboard and on-brand images (Lab 7) | A free option is enough |
| **An AI-enabled design tool** — Canva, Microsoft Copilot / Designer, or your image tool | Combining copy and image into launch visuals (Lab 8) | Optional but useful; any one is enough |
| **Internet connection** | Every generative feature runs in the cloud | Required for every step |

## What each tool does in this course

| Tool | What you do with it | Labs |
|---|---|---|
| **ChatGPT / Claude / Gemini / Copilot** | Ideate, define brand voice, draft the story and copy, adapt for audiences, brainstorm and mind-map, challenge and unblock, write and rehearse the pitch | 1–6, 9–11 |
| **An AI image tool** (DALL·E / Gemini / Copilot / Firefly / Canva) | Generate the moodboard and on-brand hero and lifestyle images | 1, 7 |
| **An AI-enabled design tool** (Canva / Copilot Designer / image tool) | Combine copy and image into a launch visual and a presentation slide | 8 |

## The AI workflow you'll use

Every lab follows the same loop — **generate → review → refine** — and the course
as a whole follows one workflow:

**Set the brief → Ideate → Create → Refine → Present.**

You set the brief, define the voice, and bring the taste, judgement and
originality; the AI generates, drafts and visualises; you curate, transform and
own every result.

## Before Lab 1 — confirm your setup

1. Open and **sign in** to at least one chat assistant (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Copilot).
2. Open and **sign in** to an AI image tool (DALL·E in ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot / Designer, Firefly or Canva).
3. Send a simple prompt (for example *"Hello, are you ready to help me build a creative campaign?"*) and confirm you get a reply.
4. Create a project folder called **`Island-Botanica-Campaign`** for all your work.
5. Open the **Lumina Botanicals brief** in [reference-pack/](reference-pack/).

If any of these is missing, tell the trainer at the start of the day.

## Responsible & original use

- Do **not** paste confidential brand material, personal information or unpublished work into a public AI tool — use the supplied Lumina Botanicals brief.
- Treat every AI output — **especially copy and images** — as a first draft to review, fact-check and make original. Don't pass off AI output as wholly your own without transforming it.
- Do **not** imitate a real brand's or a living artist's protected look or voice, and check your image tool's rules on ownership and commercial use.
- Be transparent about AI assistance (including AI-generated images) where it matters, before the work goes to a real audience.

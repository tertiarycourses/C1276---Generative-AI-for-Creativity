# C1276 — Generative AI for Creativity

Unlock your creative potential with generative AI. This one-day, hands-on course teaches you to use
generative AI — general chat assistants (**ChatGPT**, **Claude**, **Gemini**, **Microsoft Copilot**) and
an **AI image tool** (DALL·E, Gemini, Copilot / Designer, Adobe Firefly, Canva) — across the entire
creative process: setting up an AI creative toolkit and prompt library, defining a brand's tone, style
and voice, creating responsibly, drafting stories and marketing copy, adapting content for different
audiences, generating visual concepts and images, building social and launch content, brainstorming
big ideas, breaking creative blocks, combining tools into a repeatable workflow, and pitching the
result — while keeping the ideas, the voice and the originality firmly your own.

## One Connected Project

Every lab builds one **connected deliverable** — the **Island Botanica launch campaign** for Lumina
Botanicals, a fictional Singapore artisanal home-fragrance brand releasing a limited-edition botanical
collection. You start in Lab 1 by setting up an AI creative toolkit and prompt library, and across the
day take the campaign all the way to a rehearsed creative pitch by Lab 11: a locked brand tone, style
and voice, a responsible-use check, a brand story and marketing copy, copy adapted for every audience
and channel, a visual moodboard and on-brand images, a social and launch content kit, a brainstormed
mind map, a challenged and strengthened campaign concept, and a repeatable creative workflow. A Lumina
Botanicals brief is supplied in [`labs/reference-pack/`](labs/reference-pack/).

## Topics

1. **Getting Started with Generative AI for Creative Work** — overview of generative AI tools (ChatGPT,
   Claude, Gemini, Copilot); how generative AI supports the creative process; prompt engineering
   essentials for creative output; setting tone, style and voice; copyright, originality and responsible
   use. *(Labs 1–4)*
2. **Creating Written and Visual Content with AI** — drafting stories, scripts and marketing copy;
   rewriting, polishing and adapting for different audiences; generating images and visual concepts with
   AI image tools; creating social media and presentation visuals; iterating with follow-up prompts.
   *(Labs 5–8)*
3. **AI-Assisted Ideation and Creative Workflows** — brainstorming ideas and building mind maps;
   challenging conventional thinking and exploring alternatives; overcoming creative blocks; combining
   multiple AI tools into a creative workflow; presenting and pitching creative ideas with AI support.
   *(Labs 9–11)*
